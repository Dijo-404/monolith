`MONOLITH_DEPLOYED_UI_ORIGIN` to the actual canonical HTTPS origin, omit
`MONOLITH_PREVIEW_URL`, and use a new output directory. Source and compiled inputs
must be clean and frozen, and deployment provenance must be checked first.

This is an intermediate actual HTTPS observation. It does not include the
separately owned MN-016 presenter mount or subsequently accepted transport changes;
the final assembled release needs its own build, deployed capture and acceptance.
It does not automatically accept MN-014, MN-025, MN-016 or MN-017, establish sponsor
eligibility, or promise a hosting SLA or universal anonymity.

The endpoint, observer and tunnel provider can see source or RPC payloads; their
operational retention is unverified. Ordinary hosting, allocation, registry,
diagnostic and worker-bootstrap contacts are disclosed outside app RPC coverage.
Worker WebRTC/KPS, wallets, extensions, unrelated pages and arbitrary applications
are outside this capture. Stage hops are illustrative. The free backend depends
on the workstation, bounded service lifetime and external providers.
